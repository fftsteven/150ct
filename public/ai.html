Use 150ct in ChatGPT and Claude

Ask your AI app for a potluck sheet, a date poll, a Secret Santa draw or a gift registry, and it makes a real 150ct link for you to send to the group. Everyone still picks for themselves on the link.

Add it

Server address: https://150ct.com/mcp. No sign-in or API key.

  • Claude: Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector, then paste the address.
  • ChatGPT: Settings, Apps, turn on developer mode, then create an app with the address and no authentication.
  • Other apps: anything that supports remote MCP servers over Streamable HTTP.

What you can ask

  • "Set up a Secret Santa for Ana, Ben, Cy, Dee, Eve and Fay, $30 limit. Ana and Ben are a couple."
  • "Make a Friendsgiving potluck sheet for December 5 with turkey, pie, rolls and salad."
  • "Start a poll for dinner next Thursday, Friday or Saturday."
  • "Make a baby shower registry with these links."
  • "Make a football squares grid for the game, $5 a square."
  • "How many grams is 2/3 cup of brown sugar?"

Tools

ToolWhat it does
create_potluckPotluck sign-up sheet. Guests claim dishes so nothing doubles up.
create_date_pollWhen works poll with days or hours. Guests tap when they're free.
create_gift_exchangeDraws Secret Santa names. Each person taps their name to see who they're buying for. Nobody sees the pairs, including the AI.
create_registryGift list from any store. Guests tap a gift so nothing is bought twice.
create_squaresFootball squares grid. Guests tap their own squares, the host locks it to draw the numbers. No money handled.
cups_to_gramsConverts cups and spoons of 21 baking ingredients to grams. Read only.

Each link tool gives back a link for the group and a private host link for you. The host link lets you edit or delete it, so keep it to yourself.

Privacy

The AI app sends 150ct only what goes into the link, like the event name, date and names. It's the same as making the link on the site, and it deletes itself after the event. Your chat is never sent to us. See the Privacy Policy.
