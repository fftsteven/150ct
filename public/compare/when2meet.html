A When2meet alternative that's easy on a phone

Updated November 26, 2026

150ct's When works is a free When2meet alternative made for phones. You pick some days, send one link to the group chat, everyone taps when they're free, and the best day shows at the top. Like When2meet, nobody signs up.

When 150ct fits

Friends or family picking a night for dinner, a trip or a game night, mostly on their phones from a group chat. You care about which day works, and maybe a rough time.

When When2meet fits

Work or school groups finding a precise meeting slot across a whole week, where most people fill it in on a laptop.

How to use When works

  1. Open 150ct.com/meet and pick the possible days.
  2. Add hours if the time matters.
  3. Send the link. The best day shows at the top as people answer.

Questions

Is 150ct free like When2meet?

Yes. It's free with no ads, and nobody signs up.

Can I poll for times, not just days?

Yes. Pick days, then add hours. Everyone taps the hours they're free.

Does the link expire?

It deletes itself 30 days after the last day in the poll.
