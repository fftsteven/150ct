A SignUpGenius alternative for potlucks

Updated November 26, 2026

For a potluck, 150ct is a simpler SignUpGenius alternative: list the dishes, send one link, and guests claim what they're bringing. Nobody makes an account, including the host, and there are no ads. SignUpGenius is the better pick for big recurring sign ups with reminders and payments.

When 150ct fits

A one time potluck, Friendsgiving or office lunch where you want a list up in a minute and guests in a group chat to claim a dish without signing in.

When SignUpGenius fits

Schools, teams and volunteer groups running many sign ups through the year, who need reminders, time slots and payments.

How to make a potluck list

  1. Open 150ct.com/potluck and start from a template or type your dishes.
  2. Send the link to the group chat.
  3. Guests tap a dish to claim it. Once it's taken, it's taken for everyone.
