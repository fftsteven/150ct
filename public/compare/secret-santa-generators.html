Which one to pick

  • Everyone's in one group chat: 150ct. Type the names, send one link, done in a minute. Nobody, not even the organizer, sees the pairs.
  • Your group loves wishlists: Elfster or DrawNames. Both let people build lists with gift ideas and links.
  • A big group that should sign itself up: DrawNames, where people join from an invite link.
  • Everyone's on email: Secret Santa Organizer is basic, but it emails each person their match.
  • Everyone's in the same room: Names in a hat still works. Redraw if someone pulls their own.

The one thing to check

Ask who can see the pairs. On some tools the organizer can look up every match, which is fine until the organizer wants to play too. On 150ct each person only sees their own match, and the organizer only sees who has looked.
