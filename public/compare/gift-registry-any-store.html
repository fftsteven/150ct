Why no account matters

Guests are often grandparents, coworkers or friends of friends. Every account or app between them and the gift is a place they give up and text you instead. On 150ct guests open a link, tap a gift, and buy it wherever it's sold.

Questions

Is there a free registry that works with any store and needs no account?

Yes. On 150ct you paste links from any store, send one link, and guests tap the gift they'll get. Nobody makes an account.

How does a registry stop duplicate gifts?

A guest marks the gift they're getting so it shows as taken for everyone else. On 150ct that's one tap.

Can I keep the gifts a surprise?

On 150ct, yes. Turn on surprise mode and you won't see what's been claimed. Guests still see what's taken.
