Which one to pick

  • A Friendsgiving or party in a group chat: 150ct. The list is up in a minute and guests tap a dish.
  • A school, team or volunteer group: SignUpGenius or SignUp.com, for reminders, time slots and many sign ups through the year.
  • A group that coordinates by email: PerfectPotluck, which emails confirmations and reminders.
  • You already live in Google Drive: A shared sheet works, but anyone can overwrite anyone, and on a phone it's fiddly.

Questions

What's the easiest free potluck sign up sheet?

For a group chat, 150ct: type the dishes or start from a template, send one link, and guests tap what they're bringing. No accounts or emails.

How do I stop people bringing the same dish?

Use a list where each dish can only be claimed once. On 150ct, once a dish is taken it shows as taken for everyone.

Which potluck sign ups send reminders?

SignUpGenius, SignUp.com and PerfectPotluck send email reminders. 150ct doesn't, so a quick message in the group chat does the job.
