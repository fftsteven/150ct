150ct vs Elfster for Secret Santa

Updated November 26, 2026

If you just want to draw names and keep them secret, 150ct is quicker: type the names, send one link, and each person taps their name. Elfster is the better pick if your group wants wishlists, shopping and reminders in one place, and doesn't mind making accounts.

When 150ct fits

A family or office group where some people won't sign up for anything, or don't check email. Everyone is already in one group chat, and you want names drawn in a minute without you knowing every pair.

When Elfster fits

A group that likes building wishlists, wants shopping links and reminders, and is happy to make accounts. It's also handy if you run the same exchange every year and want the history.

How to switch for this year

  1. Open 150ct.com/gift and type everyone's name.
  2. Add a budget and swap date if you want, and any couples to keep apart.
  3. Send the link to the group chat. Each person taps their own name to see who they got.

Questions

Is 150ct free like Elfster?

Yes. 150ct is free with no ads, and nobody needs an account or an email.

Can the organizer see the pairs on 150ct?

No. Each person only sees their own match. The organizer can see who has looked, never who has who.

Does 150ct have wishlists?

Each person can add one line, like sizes or things they like. For full wishlists with shopping links, Elfster does more.
