Free group scheduling polls compared

Updated November 28, 2026

For friends picking a day from a group chat, 150ct's When works is the quickest on a phone. For precise time slots across a week, When2meet or Crab Fit. For work meetings tied to calendars, Doodle. For a Doodle style date poll without ads, Rallly.

Which one to pick

  • A dinner, trip or game night: 150ct. Pick days, send the link, and the best day shows at the top with who can't make it.
  • A study group or team finding an hour: When2meet or Crab Fit. The drag grid is great for 15 or 30 minute slots, especially on a laptop.
  • Meetings at work: Doodle, for calendar connections and booking pages.
  • A few fixed options to vote on: Rallly, which is free and lets people vote without an account.

When works on 150ct. Pick some days, everyone taps when they're free. Free, no sign up.

Questions

What's the easiest free way to find a date for a group?

Send one poll link to the group chat. On 150ct you pick the days, everyone taps when they're free, and the best day shows at the top.

Which scheduling polls don't need an account?

150ct, When2meet, Rallly and Crab Fit let you make a poll without an account. Doodle asks the organizer for a free account.

Which works best on a phone?

150ct is built for tapping on a phone. Grid tools like When2meet work best on a laptop.
