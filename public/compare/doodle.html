150ct vs Doodle for picking a date with friends

Updated November 28, 2026

For friends or family picking a day, 150ct's When works is lighter than Doodle: you don't make an account, there are no ads, and the best day shows at the top. Doodle is the better pick for work, where calendar connections and booking pages matter.

When 150ct fits

A dinner, trip, game night or reunion where the poll goes into a group chat and people answer on their phones. You mostly care which day works, maybe a rough time.

When Doodle fits

Meetings at work, where people want the poll to check their calendar, you run polls every week, or you also want a booking page for one on one meetings.

How to make a poll on 150ct

  1. Open 150ct.com/meet and pick the possible days.
  2. Add hours if the time matters.
  3. Send the link. The best day shows at the top as people answer.

When works on 150ct. Pick some days, everyone taps when they're free. Free, no sign up.

Questions

Do I need an account to make a poll on 150ct?

No. Nobody makes an account, not you and not the people answering.

Does 150ct have ads?

No. 150ct has no ads.

Can I see who can't make the best day?

Yes. The best day shows at the top, with the names of anyone who can't make it.
