150ct vs PerfectPotluck

Updated November 28, 2026

Both are free and neither makes anyone create an account. PerfectPotluck sends email confirmations and reminders, so it suits a group that lives in email. 150ct is made for a group chat: guests tap a dish on their phone and type their name, with no email asked.

When 150ct fits

A Friendsgiving, office lunch or backyard party where the link goes in a group chat. You want the list up in a minute and guests claiming a dish without giving out an email.

When PerfectPotluck fits

A church, club or neighborhood group that coordinates by email and likes a reminder the day before. It's also been around a long time, so some groups already know it.

How to make a potluck list on 150ct

  1. Open 150ct.com/potluck and start from a template or type your dishes.
  2. Send the link to the group chat.
  3. Guests tap a dish to claim it. Once it's taken, it's taken for everyone.

Questions

Do guests need to give an email on 150ct?

No. Guests type a name and tap a dish. That's it.

Can guests add a dish that isn't on the list?

Yes. Guests can add their own dish and it shows up for everyone.

Does 150ct send reminders?

No. If you want a reminder, send one in the group chat. For automatic email reminders, PerfectPotluck or SignUpGenius do that.
