Office potluck tips

  • Ask for one vegetarian main and label anything with nuts.
  • Check how many outlets and how much fridge space there is before people bring slow cookers.
  • Send the list about a week ahead, and put mains and sides at the top. The easy dishes go first.

Questions

What should I bring to a Christmas potluck?

Check the host's list first. Sides like mashed potatoes, roasted vegetables and rolls are always useful, and cookies or a pie are an easy dessert.

What's a good office holiday potluck list?

Two mains like a ham and a baked pasta, three sides, a salad, rolls, two desserts, drinks, plus plates, napkins and serving spoons. Ask for one vegetarian main.

How do I stop people bringing the same dish?

Use a list where each dish can be claimed once. 150ct's Potluck does this for free with one link and no sign-up. On paper, have people write their name next to a dish instead of adding a new line.
