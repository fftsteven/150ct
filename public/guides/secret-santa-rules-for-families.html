Secret Santa rules for families

Updated November 25, 2026

Keep family Secret Santa rules short: one budget, one swap date, nobody draws their own partner, and everyone writes a one-line wishlist. Draw names so no one, including the organizer, knows the pairs.

The core rules

  1. One budget for everyone. Pick a number that works for the person with the tightest budget. $25 to $50 is common for families.
  2. One swap date. Usually the day the family gets together. If some people are far away, set a ship-by date a week earlier.
  3. No drawing your own partner or kids. You're already buying for them. Mark these pairs to keep apart before the draw.
  4. Everyone writes a wishlist line. Even "surprise me" helps. It saves the classic candle and gift card swap.
  5. Keep it secret until the swap. Draw names in a way where only each person sees their own match.

Including kids

Young kids can be in the draw, with a parent helping them shop. If you'd rather keep kids separate, run two exchanges: one for the adults and one for the cousins, each with its own budget.

Big families

For 15 or more people, Secret Santa is the easiest way to keep gift giving manageable, since everyone buys one great gift instead of a dozen small ones. Some families also add a rule that you can't draw the same person two years in a row. Write last year's pairs down and keep them apart when you draw.

When someone drops out

If someone drops out before they've looked, just reset and draw again. If people have already seen their matches, the simplest fix is for the organizer to buy the gift for whoever was left without a Santa.

Fun twists

  • A theme, like "something handmade" or "a book and a snack."
  • Guess your Santa when the gifts are opened.
  • White elephant round after Secret Santa, with a small separate budget.

Questions

What is a good Secret Santa budget for family?

$25 to $50 is common. Pick the number that works for the person with the tightest budget, and say it up front.

Should couples be allowed to draw each other?

Usually not, since they already buy for each other. Mark them to keep apart when you draw names.

How do we do Secret Santa if the family lives far apart?

Draw names online with one link, set a ship-by date a week before you meet, and have everyone add a one-line wishlist.
