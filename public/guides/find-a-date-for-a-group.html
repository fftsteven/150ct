How to find a date that works for a big group

Updated November 25, 2026

Pick a handful of possible days, send one link, and have everyone tap the days they're free. The day that works for the most people shows up on top, so you can lock it in.

Why the group chat never decides

"What works for everyone?" gets ten answers in ten formats. Someone says weekends, someone else says "not the 14th," and the thread moves on before anyone adds it up. You need everyone's answer in one place, counted for you.

The fast way

  1. Pick 5 to 10 real options. Fewer choices means faster answers. Skip days you already know won't work.
  2. Add hours only if you need to. For dinner or a party, days are usually enough. For a call, add a time window.
  3. Send one link. Everyone taps the days they're free. No account needed.
  4. Watch the best day. It's shown on top with who's in and who can't make it.
  5. Pick and announce it. Once most people have answered, lock it in. Waiting for the last person is how plans die.

Tips for big groups

  • Set a deadline. "Tap your days by Friday" gets answers faster than an open-ended ask.
  • Decide who's essential. If the guest of honor can't make a day, it's out, no matter how many others can.
  • Go with most, not all. With 10 or more people, a day that works for 80% is a win.
