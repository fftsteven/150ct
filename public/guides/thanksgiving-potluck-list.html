Thanksgiving and Friendsgiving potluck sign-up list

Updated November 25, 2026

A classic Thanksgiving potluck needs turkey, stuffing, mashed potatoes, gravy, a green vegetable, cranberry sauce, rolls, a salad and pie. Put them on a sign-up list where each dish can be claimed once, and share it as one link.

The list

  • Main: Turkey (usually the host)
  • Sides: Stuffing, Mashed potatoes, Gravy, Mac and cheese, Green bean casserole, Sweet potatoes, Roasted vegetables
  • Also: Cranberry sauce, Dinner rolls, Salad
  • Dessert: Pumpkin pie, Apple pie, Something chocolate
  • Drinks: Wine, Cider, Sparkling water
  • Easy to forget: Ice, Whipped cream, Butter, Foil and containers for leftovers

150ct's Potluck has this list as a ready template. Pick Thanksgiving, change what you want, and send the link.

Friendsgiving changes

  • Swap the whole turkey for a turkey breast or a ham if the group is small.
  • Add a vegetarian main so everyone has a full plate.
  • Let people add the dish they're known for. That's half the fun.

Timing

Send the list a week ahead so people can plan. If it's last minute, send it anyway. A list that shows what's still open fills up fast, and the easy dishes go first, so put the main and sides at the top.

Questions

What should I bring to a Thanksgiving potluck?

Check the host's list first. Sides like mashed potatoes, stuffing, mac and cheese and green beans are always useful, and pie is the classic dessert.

What's a good Friendsgiving potluck list?

Turkey breast or ham, stuffing, mashed potatoes, gravy, a green vegetable, mac and cheese, rolls, a salad, a vegetarian main, pie and drinks.
