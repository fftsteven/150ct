How to do Secret Santa without anyone knowing who has who

Updated November 25, 2026

Use a name drawer that shows each person only their own match. Type everyone's name, send one link, and each person taps their name to see who they're buying for. Nobody else sees the pairs, including you.

Why pulling names from a hat doesn't work anymore

A hat only works when everyone is in the same room. When half the group is remote, someone usually ends up drawing for the people who aren't there, and now one person knows several matches. Organizers often draw everyone's names themselves and text each person, which means the organizer knows every pair and can't be part of the surprise.

The simplest way that keeps it secret

  1. List everyone. Type each person's name, including your own if you're in it.
  2. Add your rules. Set a budget and a swap date if you want, and mark anyone who shouldn't draw each other, like couples or roommates.
  3. Draw. The names are drawn right away. No one draws themselves.
  4. Send one link. Drop it in the group chat.
  5. Everyone taps their own name. They see who they're buying for. Their name then locks to their phone, so no one can check someone else's.

As the organizer you can see who has opened theirs, so you know who to nudge. You can't see who has who.

What if someone taps the wrong name?

The organizer can reset that one name. The person who tapped it by mistake opens the link again and picks the right name. The draw itself doesn't change, so no one else is affected.

Tips that keep it fun

  • Set the budget up front. A clear number like $25 avoids one person spending $10 and another spending $80.
  • Ask for a wishlist line. One line like "coffee, socks, anything blue" saves everyone from guessing.
  • Pick the swap date before you draw, so people know how long they have to shop or ship.
