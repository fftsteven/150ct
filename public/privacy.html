Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies. We don't sell or share your data.
  • We keep only what you type into a link, and it's deleted automatically, generally 30 days after the event.
  • Anyone with a link can see what's in it, so share links with care.

What we collect

  • What you type into a link. For example an event name, date, list of dishes, people's names, which days work, wishlists, which name you picked in a gift exchange, gift registry items and links, and which gifts you said you'd get. Hosts can enter other people's first names; please only add what the group needs.
  • A random ID in your browser. Your browser's local storage keeps a random ID and the name you last used, so a link remembers you. It also keeps host keys for links you made. This stays on your device. It's not a cookie and isn't used to track you anywhere else.
  • Host keys. We store only a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of each host key.
  • Where to eat. Places you save are kept only in your browser. When you search for a place or paste a Google Maps link, the search text or link is sent to Google to look it up. We don't save it.
  • Abuse protection. To stop spam we count requests per IP address per hour. We keep only a scrambled version of the IP, and the counter deletes itself within an hour.
  • Visit counts. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and visitors. It doesn't use cookies, and it doesn't identify you or follow you across sites.
  • Hosting logs. Our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically records basic request data such as IP address, browser type and time, to run and secure the site, and keeps it for a limited time under its own policies.
  • Email. If you email us, we get your address and message and use them only to reply.

We don't collect payment details (tips go through Ko-fi), precise location, contacts, or anything from social accounts.

How we use it

Only to run the Service: to show a link's contents to the people it's shared with, to keep the site working and secure, to stop abuse, and to understand overall usage. We don't sell, rent or share personal information for advertising, and we don't use it to build profiles.

Who can see it

  • People with the link. Anyone who has a link can see what's in it, such as names and answers. A Gift exchange is the exception: no one, including the host and us through the Service, is shown who drew whom, and a wishlist is shown only to the person who drew you.
  • Service providers who run parts of the Service for us: Vercel (hosting and analytics), Upstash (database), Google (place search and fonts) and Ko-fi (tips, only if you click through). They process data under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • The law. We may disclose information if we believe it's required by law, or needed to protect someone's safety or the Service's security.

How long we keep it

Links and their responses delete themselves automatically, generally 30 days after the event date (a gift exchange without a date lasts about 60 days, a registry about 90). A host can delete a link, and remove a person's response, at any time. Abuse counters last up to an hour. Data in your browser stays until you clear it.

Your choices and rights

You can clear your browser's site data to remove your ID and saved places. If you want something removed from a link and the host can't do it, email 150countapp@gmail.com with the link and we'll help. Because there are no accounts, we may not be able to tell which data is yours without the link.

Depending on where you live (including California and the EU/UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict how it's used. Email us to make a request. We'll respond within the time the law requires and won't treat you differently for asking. We don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law, and we don't use sensitive personal information to infer anything about you.

Children

150ct isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security and location

Data is sent over HTTPS, and links and host keys are long random codes that are hard to guess. No system is perfectly secure, so don't put sensitive information in a link. The Service is run from the United States, and your data may be processed there.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. Using the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
