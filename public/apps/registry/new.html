Questions

Is this gift registry free?

Yes. It's free with no ads, and nobody needs an account, an email or an app, not you and not your guests.

Can I add gifts from any store?

Yes. Paste a link from any store, like Amazon, Target, Etsy or a small shop, and add a price if you want. Guests tap the link to buy it wherever it's sold.

Will two people buy the same gift?

No. When a guest taps I'll get it, that gift shows as taken for everyone else, so nothing gets bought twice.

Can I keep it a surprise?

Yes. Turn on Keep it a surprise and you won't see what's been claimed or by whom. Guests still see what's taken. You can turn it off later if you want to see who got what for thank-you notes.

How long does the link last?

It deletes itself 30 days after the event date, or about 90 days after you make it if there's no date. You can delete it sooner.
