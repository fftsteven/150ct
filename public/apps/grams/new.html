    Questions

    How many grams is a cup of flour?

    About 120 grams for all-purpose flour, spooned into the cup and leveled. Bread flour is also about 120 grams, and whole wheat flour about 113 grams.

    How many grams is a cup of sugar?

    About 198 grams of granulated sugar. Brown sugar, packed, is about 213 grams, and powdered sugar about 113 grams.

    How many grams is a cup of butter?

    227 grams, which is two US sticks. One tablespoon of butter is about 14 grams.

    Where do the numbers come from?

    Flours, sugars, oats, cocoa, cornstarch and chocolate chips follow the King Arthur Baking ingredient weight chart, which is what most US recipes are written against. Liquids, fats, rice and salt use USDA standard cup weights.
