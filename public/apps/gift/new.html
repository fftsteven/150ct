Questions

Is this Secret Santa generator free?

Yes. It's free with no ads, and nobody needs an account, an email or an app.

Can the organizer see who has who?

No. The pairs are drawn on the server and each person only sees their own match. The organizer can see who has looked, never who has who.

Can I keep couples from drawing each other?

Yes. Add them as a pair to keep apart, and the draw makes sure they don't get each other. Nobody ever draws themselves.

What if someone taps the wrong name?

The organizer can reset that one name so the right person can open it. The draw itself stays the same.

How long does the link last?

It deletes itself 30 days after the swap date. The organizer can delete it sooner.
