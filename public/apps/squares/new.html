Questions

How do football squares work?

There's a 10 by 10 grid with one team across the top and the other down the side. People pick their squares, then the numbers 0 to 9 are drawn at random for each side. After each quarter, the last digit of each team's score points to one winning square.

Can I use this for a Super Bowl squares pool?

Yes. Make a grid before the Super Bowl, send the link to the group chat, and everyone picks their own squares from their phone. Enter the score after each quarter and the winning square lights up.

Is it free?

Yes. It's free with no ads, and nobody needs an account, an email or an app.

Does 150ct handle the money?

No. The cost per square is just text on the grid. 150ct never collects or pays out anything. It shows the grid and who won, and your group settles up on its own.

When are the numbers drawn?

When the host locks the grid. The numbers are drawn at random on the server, so nobody picks them, and squares can't change after that.

Can the host fill in squares for someone?

Yes. The host can type someone else's name and tap squares for them, handy for people who aren't on their phone.

How long does the link last?

It deletes itself 30 days after game day. The host can delete it sooner.
